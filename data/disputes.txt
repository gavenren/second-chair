# Dispute bases. One block per base. Fields:
#   == id | domain | amount in dispute (dollars)
#   A: name | role noun | how B refers to A      (A is the party the disputed money would go to)
#   B: name | role noun | how A refers to B
#   STAKE: what the disputed amount is
#   BG: agreed background.  A1,A2: facts that help A.  B1,B2: facts that help B.
#   DA / DB / DM: the decisive fact in the A-favoured, B-favoured and mixed versions.
